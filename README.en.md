## From the floor. For the floor.

Not just another poker app. Keep the clock, entries and prize pool in one workspace. Prepare your tournament, record player counts and rebuys, and control the clock. Players see the time, blinds and payouts on a second screen.

Turniermanager Lite is a local desktop app for regular NLHE tournaments using anonymous player counts. You can:

- prepare reusable templates with blinds, breaks and the relevant rebuy options;
- record entries and purchases as quantities, and reverse a specific transaction;
- control the clock, levels and breaks, with manual time correction when needed;
- keep prize contributions separate from fees and review payout ranges;
- run a player display on a second screen and create local backups.

Arne Franz has been active in live poker for more than 15 years and draws on his experience as a floorman. That practice shapes the concept, tournament workflows and product architecture. AI assists the software implementation.
